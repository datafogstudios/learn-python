What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and understand how you use Learn Python.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for the site to function. Cannot be disabled. These remember your login session and security preferences.

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

Help us understand which lessons are popular and how students navigate the curriculum. We use anonymous data only.

Preference Cookies (Optional)

Remember your theme, font size, and lesson progress.

Managing Cookies

You can disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your learning experience.
