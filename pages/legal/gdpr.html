GDPR Policy

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA)

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases: Consent (analytics, marketing emails), Contract (account management), and Legitimate Interests (site security, fraud prevention).

Your GDPR Rights

→
Right of Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
→
Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data.
→
Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
→
Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data.
→
Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
→
Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Data Transfers

If we transfer your data outside the EEA, we ensure adequate safeguards are in place via Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., ICO in the UK, CNIL in France).
