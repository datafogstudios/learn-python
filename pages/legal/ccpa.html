CCPA Notice

For California Residents — California Consumer Privacy Act

Your California Rights

California residents have the following rights under the CCPA:

→
Right to Know: What personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell.
→
Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information (with certain exceptions).
→
Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.
→
Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

Do Not Sell My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties. If this changes, we will provide a clear "Do Not Sell" opt-out mechanism and update this notice.

How to Submit a Request

Email: privacy@learnpython.com
We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days.

Authorized Agent

You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. The agent must provide written authorization and you must verify your identity directly with us.
